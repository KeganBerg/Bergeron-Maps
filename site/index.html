How it works

Live in an afternoon.
Learned in a minute.

  1. 01Build the map

    Answer four questions and get a full, numbered lot. Or upload your hand sketch and trace over it. Floors, ramps and exits included.

  2. 02Publish it to your team

    Invite managers and attendants by email. Each person sees only the sites they work at. Edits stay in draft until you publish.

  3. 03Find any car

    Attendants type a space number on their phone. It lights up, with the floor and the way there. No app to install.
