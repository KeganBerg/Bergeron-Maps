Common questions

Signing in

Do I need a password?

No. Type your work email on the sign-in page. We email you a sign-in link and a six-digit code. Open the link, or type the code. Both work for one hour.

I did not get the sign-in email.

Wait a minute, then look in your spam or junk folder. Check that you typed the email your account was set up with. You can ask for a new email on the same page.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Bergeron Maps works in the web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Go to bergeronmaps.com and choose Sign in.

Building your map

What is the fastest way to start a map?

Choose Build it for me when you add a map. Answer four questions: floors, rows per floor, spaces per row and accessible spaces. Then change anything that is different at your site.

Can I use my paper drawing?

Yes. Take a photo of it with your phone. In the editor, open the Layers tab and choose Upload site plan or aerial photo. Then draw over it.

Do I need to save?

No. The map saves as you work. You see Saved at the top of the editor.

I made a mistake. Can I go back?

Yes. Use Undo for a recent change. Saved versions of the map let you go back to an earlier point.

Publishing

Why can my team not see my changes?

Your changes stay in draft until you choose Publish changes. Your team keeps seeing the last published map until then.

How do I check the map before my team sees it?

Choose Preview in the editor. It shows exactly what your employees will see.

Your team

How do employees get on the map?

Give them your site code. It has six characters. They sign in, type the code under Join a site, and choose Join site. Employees can look at the map but cannot change it.

How do I add a manager?

Open Sites & people and choose Give someone access. Type their work email, pick their access and the site. The app does not send them an email, so copy the message it gives you and send it yourself.

Someone left the company. What do I do?

Open Sites & people, find them under People, and choose Remove. They lose access at once.

Our site code was shared too widely.

Open Sites & people and choose New code. The old code stops working. People who already joined keep their access.
